Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our service.

We deliver every day from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. You can choose a convenient delivery slot during checkout.

There is no minimum order value. However, a nominal delivery fee is applied to orders below ₹299.

You can track your order in real-time from the "My Orders" section in your account once it has been dispatched.

We have a no-questions-asked return policy for all perishable items at the time of delivery. For other items, you can initiate a return within 24 hours if the product is damaged or incorrect.
